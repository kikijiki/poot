# The Megakernel

A megakernel combines work from several kernels into one persistent device launch. Extending it to
an entire token loop also requires on-device sampling and stop handling. Related persistent-kernel
work includes Mirage [^mirage] and MPK [^mpk].

poot has no megakernel. The kernel IR has scoped fences and compare-and-swap, and the PTX and ROCm runtimes expose
cooperative-launch mechanisms. Those pieces do not constitute a megakernel: a recorder alone cannot
make arbitrary host-loaded kernels callable from a persistent kernel, and a multi-token loop needs
sampling, cancellation and streaming contracts. The SPIR-V backends, wgpu and raw Vulkan, have no
grid-wide synchronization primitive, which this strategy would require. Execution is
[native replay or cached dispatch encoding](./execution).

[^mirage]: Wu, M. et al., "Mirage: A Multi-Level Superoptimizer for Tensor Programs," OSDI, 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05751

[^mpk]: Wu, M. et al., "MPK: A Compiler and Runtime for Mega-Kernelizing Tensor Programs," arXiv:2512.22219, 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22219
